# GiftQuest Flutter — Working Rules

## Testing boundary
NEVER run the app, use adb commands, launch emulators, or attempt any
device/UI testing. This is strictly off-limits regardless of what seems
convenient in the moment. Testing is handled entirely by the developer,
manually, after your work is done.

Your responsibility ends at:
1. Code builds without errors
2. `flutter analyze` passes clean
3. DEV_LOG.md is updated with what changed and why

Stop there. Do not attempt to verify runtime behavior yourself.
